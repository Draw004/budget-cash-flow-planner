HOW THIS WORKS

What this comparison includes — and what it does not

Same monthly paymentThe current-card path uses Carrowmont's existing tested Debt Payoff Core. The transferred path uses the same monthly timing with its promotional and later APR.
Fee and rate changes includedPercentage and fixed transfer fees are combined. Choose whether the fee is added to the transferred balance or paid in cash upfront. Both count toward the total cost.
Month-by-month planning modelMonthly interest is calculated as outstanding balance × annual APR / 12 before each payment. Rates are held fixed within each stated period; a promotional offer expires after the entered number of months.

Important limitations: This is not a card statement or product quote. Daily interest, issuer minimums, grace periods, taxes, late fees, purchase APR, new spending, credit-limit restrictions, offer termination, payment allocation and changes to variable rates are excluded. Results may differ materially from actual statements. If either path has not repaid within 50 years, Carrowmont does not present a misleading savings comparison.

Before applying: Confirm the full transfer amount including the fee fits the offered limit, read provider terms, and check whether the payment required before the promotion ends fits your budget. A lower monthly payment alone does not establish savings.

METHODOLOGY

How the calculation works

Interest is estimated monthlyEach debt uses the yearly interest rate you enter, divided into a monthly rate. Your lender's exact statement can differ if it uses daily interest, fees or another convention.
Minimum payments are paid firstThen the rest of the same monthly budget goes to the current target debt. When a debt is cleared, that freed payment moves to the next debt.
No payment is intentionally left unusedIf a debt is cleared part-way through a month, any unused amount moves to the next active debt in that same month.
